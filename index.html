The Methodology

The Willow Tree Top Talent Framework™

A Process Built for Quality, Not Volume
Standard agencies play a volume game, hoping one sticks. Willow Tree works differently.

Step 01
Deep Dive Discovery

Before sourcing candidates, we immerse ourselves in your business: your culture, growth goals, and the factors that drive leaders to succeed or fail in your environment. Most agencies skip this step; for us, it's essential.

Step 02
Competitive Positioning

We help you make the role irresistible to the best candidates by shaping a compelling offer, highlighting what sets your opportunity apart, and identifying candidates who aren't the right fit.

Step 03
Targeted Sourcing

We target top talent already excelling in their roles, leveraging our database of over 55,000 hospitality leaders and industry networks to recruit high performers who aren't actively job hunting but are the right fit for you.

Step 04
Holistic Vetting Process

We thoroughly vet each candidate for leadership ability, cultural fit, and essential traits beyond the resume. Then, we present the top 4 to 6 for you to choose from. You hire the best one.
